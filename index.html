Private backup utility

Encrypted backups, under the owner's control.

NexPOS Backup is a personal-use Google Drive integration used by the NexPOS project owner to maintain encrypted, recoverable copies of authorized database exports. It is not a public service, does not offer Google sign-in to POS customers, and does not give Google Drive access to POS users.

Setup and recovery testing · production exports not yet enabled

1 · Export privately

The owner separately authorizes and generates a database export on a trusted workstation. This is not performed through this website.

2 · Encrypt locally

Restic encrypts backup content using an owner-held recovery password before cloud transfer.

3 · Store and verify

Rclone copies the encrypted repository to a designated Google Drive folder, with independent restore and integrity checks.

What Google Drive access is used?

Our owner-operated rclone Desktop OAuth client requests the drive.file permission: it can access files it creates or that the owner explicitly opens for the app, rather than general unrestricted Google Drive access.

The cloud copy consists of encrypted backup repository files, not plaintext database exports. The operator controls retention and deletion.
