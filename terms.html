Terms of use

Effective 16 October 2026. These terms apply to this informational website and the private NexPOS Backup utility.

Purpose

NexPOS Backup is intended for the project's authorized operator to manage their own encrypted backup files in a Google Drive account they control. It is not offered as a general-purpose cloud-backup subscription service and is not a POS customer login system.

Authorization

The operator must have permission to export, encrypt, store, retrieve, and delete every dataset involved. Google account authorization is voluntary and can be revoked at Google's account permissions page.

Secure use

The operator is responsible for securing recovery passwords and OAuth credentials, independently verifying recoverability, and avoiding uploads of plaintext confidential exports. Backup availability and recovery depend on the operator's configuration and third-party services.

Service availability

This website provides information only. Backup scripts and integrations are operated separately and may change; uninterrupted service and guaranteed recovery are not promised.

Privacy

Read the privacy policy for Google Drive data access, use, storage, sharing, and deletion information.

Questions

For non-sensitive site questions, use the project issue tracker. Do not submit private data publicly.
